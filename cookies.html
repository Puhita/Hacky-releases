Cookies and privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This page explains what the Hacky website stores in your browser and who it talks to. It does not cover the apps, see the last section.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What we store in your browser

NameWhat it doesKept
hacky-themeRemembers whether you chose the light or the dark theme.In your browser’s local storage until you clear it
hacky-consentRemembers your answer to the cookie notice, so we don’t ask again on every visit.In your browser’s local storage until you clear it

Both values stay on your device and are never sent to us. They exist only to make the site work the way you asked.

Another service, only if you accept

If you press Accept in the cookie notice, the site contacts Google Fonts. If you press Decline or don’t answer, it contacts nobody, and the page uses your system fonts.

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) delivers the site’s fonts. Google receives your IP address and browser details when your browser asks for them. See Google’s privacy policy.

Links to other sites are ordinary links. Once you follow one, that site’s own rules apply.

Change your choice

You can change your answer at any time: . Clearing this site’s storage in your browser also resets it.

Hosting logs

Like any website, the host that serves these pages may keep standard request logs (such as your IP address, the page and the time) for security and operations. We don’t use them to identify visitors.

The apps

The Hacky desktop app and VS Code extension are separate from this website. They keep your login session and settings on your own computer, and they store your team’s work data in a Supabase database, as described in the terms. Your GitHub token, if you add one, stays in a local file on your computer and is not sent to Hacky’s database.
